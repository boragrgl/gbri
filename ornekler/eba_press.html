25 NOVEMBER 2026

​EBA publishes hotfix for the reporting framework v4.4 - Phase 1

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) has issued a hotfix for its Reporting Framework version 4.4 - Phase 1, addressing technical issues identified following the release of the final technical package.

25 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA examines the use of stablecoins, including EMTs, in decentralised finance

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published a Report analysing the use of EMTs and other selected stablecoins in DeFi and the implications for consumers and supervisors. The EBA finds that stablecoins, including some EMTs issued under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), play a central role in the DeFi ecosystem by providing liquidity, collateral and settlement in DeFi trading and lending activities. The Report also highlights consumer protection, and regulatory arbitrage risks arising from the growing interaction between stablecoins and DeFi.

20 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA publishes updated information on third-country groups and branches operating in the EU/EEA

The European Banking Authority (EBA) has published its 2026 update on third-country groups and third-country branches operating in the European Union and the European Economic Area (EU/EEA). The publication provides a comprehensive overview of the presence, ownership structures and organisational arrangements of third-country banking groups active across the EU/EEA, supporting transparency in the European banking sector.

19 NOVEMBER 2026

EU/EEA banks continue to display resilience amid sovereign debt pressures, borrower vulnerabilities and rising cyber threats

The EBA today published its Autumn 2026 Risk Assessment Report (RAR) together with a thematic note on lending to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and the results of its Autumn 2026 Risk Assessment Questionnaire (RAQ). The findings show that EU/EEA banks remain resilient, supported by strong capital, liquidity and profitability, while continuing to increase financing to the economy. At the same time, geopolitical uncertainty, sensitivity to sovereign debt repricing, growing interconnectedness within the financial sector and evolving operational threats warrant continued vigilance.

18 NOVEMBER 2026

​The EBA reports growing market share of third-country bank subsidiaries in the EU and reduced foreign currency mismatches

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published two Reports on the market share of subsidiaries of non-EU banks in the EU, as well as on EU banks’ assets and liabilities in foreign currencies. As of December 2025, subsidiaries of third country banking groups accounted for 10.22% of total EU banking assets, up from 9.8% a year earlier. The Reports also show that EU banks increased foreign currency funding to 25.3% of total funding, up from 24.8% as of December 2024, helping to reduce currency mismatches and largely eliminate the mismatch in US dollar positions.

16 NOVEMBER 2026

EBA supports further progress on the Banking Union, including on deposit insurance

The EBA supports further progress on the Banking Union, including on deposit insurance, as a key step towards a more efficient and resilient European banking system.

13 NOVEMBER 2026

​The EBA supports the NGFS Declaration on the macro-financial dimension of the energy transition on the occasion of COP31

​As an active member of the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS), the European Banking Authority (EBA) welcomes the publication of the NGFS Declaration on the macro-financial dimension of the energy transition on the occasion of the 2026 United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Türkiye. The Declaration highlights how energy price shocks and increasingly frequent climate-related shocks are becoming a macro-financial reality, and calls for an orderly, whole-economy transition.

9 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA streamlines the 2027 EU-wide stress test with more than 55% reduction in requested data

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today released the final methodology, draft templates, and template guidance for its 2027 EU-wide stress test, together with the key milestones for the exercise. Following extensive engagement with industry participants, the EBA has significantly streamlined the exercise, reducing the number of required data points by 55% through the simplification of reporting templates and closer alignment with supervisory reporting. These changes will reduce operational burden and improve efficiency while preserving the robustness, risk-sensitivity and comparability of stress test results. The 2027 exercise will also include, for the first time, a dedicated module assessing climate-related risks. The 2027 EU-wide stress test will formally start in January 2027, following the release of the macroeconomic and climate scenarios, with the results scheduled for publication by the end of July 2027.

9 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA publishes consumer factsheet on white-labelled financial products and services

The European Banking Authority (EBA) published today a factsheet to help consumers identify when a banking or payment product or service is white labelled, i.e. marketed under one brand but provided by another company. As consumers increasingly access financial products and services through online platforms, retailers and other non-financial brands, it may be sometimes unclear which company is responsible for providing the product or service. Understanding this distinction can help consumers make informed decisions, know who they are dealing with and reduce risks. For this reason, the factsheet also guides consumers on the key things to know and check before getting that product or service.

6 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA outlines next steps on supervisory reporting simplification and extends implementation timelines following consultation feedback on the ITS

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today outlines the way forward for the finalisation of proposals to simplify EU supervisory reporting, following extensive dialogue with the banking industry and feedback received during the public consultation. The package that is currently being finalised will introduce additional simplification measures, greater proportionality and extended implementation timelines, reducing reporting costs while preserving the information necessary for effective supervision. The final report on the draft Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) on supervisory reporting will be published by the EBA in December 2026.

5 NOVEMBER 2026

The EBA publishes revised Guidelines on internal governance, delivering greater proportionality and simplification for banks

The European Banking Authority (EBA) today published its revised Guidelines on internal governance under the Capital Requirements Directive (CRD). The revised Guidelines reflect the changes introduced by CRD VI and form part of the EBA’s broader commitment to simplify the regulatory framework, strengthen proportionality and reduce unnecessary compliance burdens, while safeguarding sound and robust governance across the EU banking sector.

29 OCTOBER 2026

The EBA advises on targeted simplifications to Taxonomy disclosures

The European Banking Authority (EBA) has published its response to the European Commission’s Call for Technical Advice on certain key performance indicators (KPIs) and other aspects of the Disclosures Delegated Act under Article 8 of the Taxonomy Regulation. The EBA advises on targeted changes to streamline disclosure requirements, reduce complexity and enhance the usability of information disclosed by credit institutions and investment firms.

23 OCTOBER 2026

​The EBA appoints Kristīne Černaja-Mežmale as its new Vice-Chair

​The Board of Supervisors of the European Banking Authority (EBA) has elected Kristīne Černaja-Mežmale as its Vice-Chair for a duration of two and a half years. Ms Černaja-Mežmale will take up her new role on 1 November 2026 when the current EBA Vice-Chair, Helmut Ettl starts his new role as Executive Director of the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

22 OCTOBER 2026

The ​EBA extends some consultation deadlines due to website maintenance​

On 21 October 2026, CERT-EU, the cybersecurity service for the EU institutions, bodies and agencies, alerted organisations to two critical security flaws in software used to run many websites, including that of the European Banking Authority (EBA). As a precaution, the EBA took its website offline on Wednesday 21 October 2026 at 13:00 CEST while the necessary security updates were applied. The website was restored on Thursday 22 October 2026 at 14:00 CEST.

14 OCTOBER 2026

​The EBA classifies USDC as the first significant e-money token in the European Union

​The European Banking Authority (EBA) has classified USDC, issued by Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, as a significant e-money token (EMT) under the Markets in Crypto Assets Regulation (MiCA), following its assessment of the criteria set out in the Regulation. There are currently 45 EMTs in the European Union, which have been notified under MiCA, and USDC is the first one to be classified as being significant.
